## Technical scope facts for EU/UK review

- The staging site is globally reachable and account creation is technically available where its prerequisites work.
- Display currencies include CAD plus optional approximate conversions such as EUR and GBP; this is convenience display, not a selected sales territory.
- Normal checkout and fulfilment are disabled. No launch shipping country/province allow-list, tax market, address validation, EU/UK localisation, EU/UK-targeted campaign, or region-specific paid marketing implementation was found.
- YouTube/Rumble frames require External media consent. Direct links remain available. No EU or UK representative is named or implemented.
- These facts do not decide GDPR/UK GDPR territorial scope. Qualified counsel must assess scope after the owner confirms launch markets, shipping, localisation and marketing plans.
