About Third Railify
Third Railify started as a bad idea that refused to die.
The third rail is the one you’re not supposed to touch. High voltage. Career-ending. The topic everybody circles and nobody grabs. We grabbed it anyway — then invited friends, built a late-night studio around it, and somehow turned “we should probably stop” into a nightly show.
We’re Shawn and Gina. Canadian. Unfiltered. Live most nights around 10pm Eastern on Rumble. One of us is the chaos agent with ADHD commentary and a habit of taking the dumbest argument way too seriously. The other is the co-host, producer, and lifeblood who keeps the train on the tracks just long enough to derail it on purpose. Together we do the thing 9-to-5 internet forgot how to do: talk like actual people, roast each other in public, and let the chat steer.
What you’ll find here isn’t a polished newsroom and it isn’t a brand-safe hangout. It’s:
Aboot Nothing — championship brackets for things that do not need a champion. Cryptids. Condiments. Cuts of beef. 90s cartoons. Positions. Chat picks. Rematches nobody asked for and everybody shows up for.
Pop Culture Beat Down — Mondays with friends who treat celebrity meltdowns, flops, and Hollywood nonsense like contact sport.
News Hangout — the news show that barely talks news, then talks everything else until the clock gives up.
Raids, wheels, merch, and whatever tangent walks in uninvited.
We are not making this up. We are also not entirely sure what “this” is on any given night. That’s the point.
If you’re tired of content that sounds like it was approved by a committee, pull up a chair. Stay for the argument. Stay for the community that actually shows up. Stay because “always be GOATED, never be mid” is less a slogan than a threat.
Grab the rail. Don’t let go.
