# Third Railify privacy operations runbook

Internal engineering/operations procedure as at 28 August 2026. This is not legal advice. Use only an authorised Admin session, the minimum identifiers necessary, and the authoritative systems below. Never copy passwords, session cookies, one-time tokens, provider credentials, raw payment data, private customer records, or unnecessary identity documents into tickets, chat, email, or this repository.

## Common intake and control steps

1. Record a case reference, request type, received time, published contact route, requested outcome, assigned authorised operator, and every action taken. Do not paste the requester's full record into the case log.
2. Ask for the least sensitive locator already associated with the feature: account email, GOATS reference/public slug, comment date and listing, or order reference/Stripe Checkout Session ID. Never request a password, full card number, OAuth credential, session cookie, CSRF token, or provider secret.
3. Verify identity proportionately through an already-associated channel or authenticated account where possible. A matching name or public profile alone is not proof. Escalate disputed, guardian, estate, representative, law-enforcement, or high-risk requests before disclosure or mutation.
4. Query Admin authority, not the Public projection or browser cache. Separate the requester's data from other people, moderator notes, audit metadata, fraud controls, provider secrets, and privileged legal material before any disclosure.
5. Preserve existing audit events. Add the case reference and a bounded action summary to the operational record; do not rewrite history. Legal hold or incident preservation overrides routine deletion only after authorised legal direction is recorded.
6. The owner must designate the accountable privacy role. Until then, route escalations through `privacy@thirdrailify.com` and obtain owner/counsel direction where this runbook identifies a blocker.

## Existing maintenance actions

- GOATS: `POST /api/admin/goats/maintenance/cleanup-drafts` is Master-only, exact-origin and CSRF-protected. It removes only `status='draft'` rows whose existing `draft_expires_at` is earlier than the operation time and deletes their R2 objects before the database row.
- Auth: `POST /api/admin/accounts/maintenance/cleanup-expired-auth` is Master-only, exact-origin and CSRF-protected. It deletes only sessions, handoffs, OAuth transactions, email-verification tokens and password-reset tokens whose existing `expires_at` is at or before the operation time. It writes a bounded auth audit event.
- Neither action is a general privacy erasure tool. Do not run it against a preservation scope.
